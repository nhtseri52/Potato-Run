      ABOUT THE GAME

      Potato Run is a free endless-runner game playable directly in your browser. Guide a running potato through spikes, birds and tall walls, break open mystery blocks for random rewards, collect coins scattered across the track, and unlock unique potato skins. Reach 1,000 points to unlock Potato Solo, a versus mode against another player or a bot.

      HOW TO PLAY

      • PC: press SPACE or click to jump; press again in mid-air for a double jump — required to clear tall walls.
      • Mobile: tap once to jump, tap twice quickly for a double jump.
      • Score increases automatically every second while you run — obstacles don't give bonus score, only coins do.
      • Mystery blocks give random rewards: bonus coins, an extra heart, a shield that clears nearby hazards, a 2x score boost, or (rarely) a heart loss — so approach them carefully!

      ACCOUNT & PROGRESS

      Playing without an account still saves your coins locally. Log in anytime and your existing coins and high score are merged into your account automatically — nothing is lost. An account is required for the global Leaderboard, Daily Missions and Potato Solo mode.
